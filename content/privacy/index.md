This website is the personal academic page of Evangelos Vlachos. The site itself does not
ask you for personal data. It uses Google Analytics to count visits.

**Personal tools.** I also register a few small tools with Google, such as the OAuth client
"rclone-compy" that backs up my own computers to my own Google Drive. These tools are used
only by me and are not offered to anyone else:

- They access only my own Google account, after I sign in and approve access.
- Data they read or write stays on my own devices and in my own Google Drive.
- Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
- Access can be revoked at any time from the Google account's security settings.
