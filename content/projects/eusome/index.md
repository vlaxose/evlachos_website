## Project Overview
**EUSOME** (Enabling Autonomous Aerial Systems through Operational Methods and Technological Enhancements) is a 4-year European Excellence Hub project funded by the **Horizon Europe** programme (Grant Agreement No. 101187121). 

With a total budget of nearly **€6 million**, the project aims to modernize the air mobility ecosystem in the Southeast Mediterranean region. It addresses regional challenges such as goods and people transportation, complex landscape surveillance, fire risk management, and traffic congestion monitoring through the integration of large drone systems and intelligent coordination technology.

## Core Pillars of Innovation
The project is structured around four primary objectives to bridge the gap between technological capability and regional implementation:

1. **Research & Innovation Infrastructure:** Establishing an open-access infrastructure to foster technological advancements in Autonomous Aerial Systems (AAS).
2. **Regulatory Masterplan:** Developing a strategic plan for the regional adoption of **EASA** (European Union Aviation Safety Agency) regulations on Advanced Air Mobility.
3. **Thematic Innovation Hub (TIH):** A dedicated hub to explore market possibilities, consultancy services, and open-access policies for project outputs.
4. **Mentoring & Outreach:** Building regional expertise through training programs and promoting public engagement to ensure societal acceptance of AAM technology.

## Project News & Milestones

* **January 2025:** Official Project Kick-off Meeting held in Cyprus, bringing together all 16 partners to align on the first phase of the AAM roadmap.
* **August 2025:** EUSOME featured at the Mediterranean Drone Forum, highlighting the regional masterplan for U-Space integration.
* **Future Milestone:** Establishment of the regional Thematic Innovation Hub (TIH) for cross-border consultancy.

## The Consortium
Coordinated by the **KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence** at the University of Cyprus, EUSOME unites a diverse team of 16 partners across Cyprus, Greece, Poland, and Serbia, including:
* **Research Institutions:** University of Cyprus (KIOS), Athena Research Center.
* **Civil Aviation Authorities:** Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (HCAA), Civil Aviation Authority of Cyprus.
* **Industry Partners:** Aviomania Aircraft, N. Karaolides Designs, HDRONES.
* **Governmental & Public Bodies:** Ministry of Digital Governance (Greece), Ministry of Transport (Cyprus).
