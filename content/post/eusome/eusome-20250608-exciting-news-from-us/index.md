 Exciting news from us! Tomorrow, on May 20th, within the framework of the EUSOME Project training and mentoring program for the Advanced Air Mobility (``) domain, the 1st mentoring visit will take place in Serbia! Led by project partner Centrum Badań Kosmicznych Polskiej Akademii Nauk, this visit aims to assess the training and mentoring needs within the consortium, especially in Serbia, with project partner Institute Mihajlo Pupin. The first visit will lay the foundation for a strong action plan to support skills development, knowledge transfer, and personnel mobility. The program focuses on engaging key Serbian players in accelerating the responsible adoption of AAM technologies, including research institutes, SMEs, and governmental stakeholders. This initiative strengthens collaboration across Europe and supports Serbia’s alignment with the EASA standards.Looking forward to a productive exchange and impactful outcomes!
