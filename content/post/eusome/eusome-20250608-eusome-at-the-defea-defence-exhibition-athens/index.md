 EUSOME at the DEFEA - Defence Exhibition Athens2025! From May 6-8, we had the chance to join one of Europe’s top defense and security events in Athens, Greece. At the Greek Ministry of Development’s exhibition area, our partners from the Industrial Systems Institute / Research Centre ATHENA proudly showcased the capabilities of our millimetre-wave radar technology, a big step forward for autonomous sensing and situational awareness in aerial systems. This cutting-edge innovation is a testament to great teamwork within the EUSOME Project consortium, including the Industrial Systems Institute / Research Centre ATHENA of the Athena Research Center and Hellenic Drones S.A.DEFEA 2025 was a fantastic opportunity to connect with industry leaders, defense experts, and innovators from around the world.
