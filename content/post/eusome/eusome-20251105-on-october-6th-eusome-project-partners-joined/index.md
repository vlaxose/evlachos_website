 On October 6th, EUSOME Project partners joined the Skysense 2025 Workshop, organized within the 11th IEEE Smart Cities Conference (October 6–9, 2025) in ``.Organized by Athena Research Center and the Computer Technology Institute and Press “DIOPHANTUS” (CTI), the workshop featured insightful presentations and discussions from experts of the EUSOME and REFINE projects, exploring how Autonomous Aerial Systems and AI-driven Environmental Monitoring are shaping smart cities and driving urban resilience. Among the speakers were:Afroditi Sakellaropoulou (Hellenic U-space Institute), Christian Vitale and Nicolas Souli (KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence), Christos Skliros (Hellenic Drones S.A.), and Evangelos Vlachos (Athena Research Center).A big thank you to all the speakers and organizers for their inspiring contributions and valuable insights!
