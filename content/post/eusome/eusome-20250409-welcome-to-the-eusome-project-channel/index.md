Welcome to the EUSOME Project Channel!We're excited to kick things off and share our project with you! EUSOME stands for Enabling Autonomous Aerial Systems through Operational Methods and Technological Enhancements. Funded by European Union's Horizon Europe programme, EUSOME is a 4-year project, dedicated to advancing Advanced Air Mobility (``) in Southeast Europe.What does this mean? We aim to address challenges in transport, emergency response, and climate adaptation to make Advanced Aerial Mobility a real part of everyday life. With 16 partners across 4 countries, we’re working together to push innovation, standardize regulations, and get the public on board with this exciting future. Throwback to our kickoff meeting, when our journey began at the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence at the University of Cyprus!Over two dynamic days, our team gathered for the first time, exchanged insights, and aligned on the road ahead.Stay tuned - there is so much more to come!EUSOME Project partners :KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence, Hellenic U-space Institute, Athena Research Center, Hellenic Ministry of Digital Governance, Cyprus Ministry of Transport Communications and Works, Hellenic Drones S.A., Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority, GRNET - Greek Research & Technology Network, Cyprus Chamber of Commerce & Industry, Adrestia R&D, Centrum Badań Kosmicznych Polskiej Akademii Nauk, SocialTech Lab, Institute Mihajlo Pupin, EPIRUS - Perifereia Ipeirou, N.
