The Hellenic Civil Aviation Authority (``) has published a UAS Flight Safety Information Bulletin in order to inform operators and users of Unmanned Aircraft Systems (``) about safe and lawful drone operations across ``.The bulletin has already been distributedto over 12,000 domestic and foreign UAV operators in Greece, with the goal of quickly and broadly distributing vital information for safe operational usage. Especially during the summer period, given the growing frequency of wildfires, particularly in forest areas, HCAA advises that using UAVs in fire-affected areas is illegal unless prior permission and coordination with the appropriate authorities have been secured. Uncontrolled drone activity can seriously interfere with aerial firefighting missions and endanger lives.Particular care is also required in locations with high local winds, which can significantly alter the speed and direction of a fire's spread, increasing the risk to ground and aircraft firefighting troops. The HCAA has already legalized the use of UAVs for fire prevention and detection, which are operated by Civil Protection at roughly 150 places across Greece, significantly improving the prompt detection and reaction to fires.
