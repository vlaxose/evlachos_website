I travelled to **Belgrade, Serbia** to participate in the **3rd EUSOME Study Visit**, hosted by our consortium partner the [Institute Mihajlo Pupin (IMP)](https://www.pupin.rs/).

The study visit brought together researchers and stakeholders from across the EUSOME consortium for a day of technical presentations, knowledge exchange, and bilateral collaboration meetings, covering advances in U-Space technologies, unmanned aerial systems, and integrated sensing and communications.

The event was organized by Dr. Valentina Janev and colleagues at IMP, and took place on **February 3, 2026**.

This visit is part of the ongoing dissemination and knowledge-transfer activities of the [EUSOME](https://eusome.eu/) project (EU Horizon Europe, Grant Agreement No. 101187121), which aims to establish an Excellence Hub bridging European 6G research and aviation regulation in the U-Space domain.
