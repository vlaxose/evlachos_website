The EUSOME mentoring and training programme officially took off in Belgrade last week! On 20 & 21 May, project partners Emil Wrzosek and Anna K. from the Centrum Badań Kosmicznych Polskiej Akademii Nauk joined the Institute Mihajlo Pupin team to kick off discussions on mentoring needs and collaboration opportunities. Huge thanks to Valentina Janev and Slavica Boštjančič Rakas for their warm welcome and seamless coordination! Day 1 focused on designing a mentoring programme tailored to the Serbian ecosystem, hosted at the IMP Technology Transfer Office Day 2 took the team to the 67th International Fair of Technics and Technical Achievements at the Belgrade Fair, where they had the chance to connect with researchers, universities, and innovative companies shaping Serbia’s tech futureThis visit marked an important step in building bridges between institutions and supporting the uptake of Advanced Air Mobility (``) and `` frameworks in the region.
