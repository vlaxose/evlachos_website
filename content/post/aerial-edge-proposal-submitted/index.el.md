We submitted **AERIAL-EDGE** to the Horizon Europe Twinning call (HORIZON-WIDERA-2026-02) today, with ATHENA RC as coordinator.

> **Full title:** AERIAL-EDGE — Twinning for Aerial Radio Measurement and Edge Data Intelligence for Mediterranean Drone Corridors

AERIAL-EDGE addresses a critical gap for U-Space deployment: the lack of calibrated, open aerial channel datasets and the edge data infrastructure to turn raw radio measurements into actionable drone corridor intelligence. The project will establish a permanent **AERIAL Lab** at ATHENA RC through structured knowledge transfer from advanced European partners.

**Consortium** (€1,500,000 total, 36 months):

- ATHENA RC, Greece (Coordinator)
- TU Ilmenau, Germany
- German Aerospace Center (DLR), Germany
- University of Sussex, UK
- KIOS Research and Innovation Centre, University of Cyprus

This work builds on ATHENA RC's aerial communications experience in the [EUSOME](https://eusome.eu/) project.

*Evaluation results expected in late 2026.*
