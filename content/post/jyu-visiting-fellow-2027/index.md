I am happy to share that I have been awarded a **Visiting Fellowship by the University of Jyväskylä (JYU)**, Finland, for a one-month research visit during 2027.

The visit will be hosted by **Prof. Markus Miettinen**, Professor of Cybersecurity at JYU. We will work on cybersecurity for UAV systems, starting with the trustworthiness of drone Remote ID, combining my work on UAV sensing and control with his group's expertise in security for AI and networked systems.
