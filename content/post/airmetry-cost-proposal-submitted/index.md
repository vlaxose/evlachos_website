We submitted **AIRMETRY** to the COST Open Call today, with me as Main Proposer for ATHENA RC.

> **Full title:** Harmonisation of Radio Sensing Measurements for Low-Altitude Airspace

As drones and other aircraft share the airspace below a few hundred metres, radio sensing (radar, passive radar and cellular integrated sensing and communication) is becoming a key tool for detecting and tracking them. Today, measurements from different testbeds are taken and reported in different ways, so they are hard to compare or reuse. AIRMETRY proposes a European network to agree on common measurement procedures, data formats and metrics, and to share them with researchers, industry and aviation authorities.
