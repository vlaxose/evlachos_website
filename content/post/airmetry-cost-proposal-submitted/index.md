We submitted **AIRMETRY** to the COST Open Call on 28 October 2026, with me as Main Proposer for ATHENA RC.

> **Full title:** Harmonisation of Radio Sensing Measurements for Low-Altitude Airspace

Research groups across Europe measure how radio links and radio sensing perform in the lowest 150 metres of airspace: air-to-ground links, the radio detection of drones, instrumented flight campaigns. Each group works under its own procedure and keeps its own data, so the results cannot be pooled. AIRMETRY proposes a European network that agrees a common metadata profile for aerial sensing data, builds a shared open database, and derives joint reference models of radio links and drone radar signatures. It also maps wireless metrics to the performance terms aviation authorities use, and trains early-career researchers across radio and aviation.

**Network of proposers:** 50 researchers from 13 countries, 9 of them COST Inclusiveness Target Countries, and 60% of the proposers are young researchers.

This work builds on ATHENA RC's aerial sensing and communications work in the [EUSOME](/projects/eusome/) project.
