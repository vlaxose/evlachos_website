Two talks in the week of 16 November 2026.

### Lisbon, 17 November: IEEE ISAC 2026

At the **first IEEE Annual Integrated Sensing and Communication Conference (ISAC 2026)** I present our paper in the session on channel estimation and synchronization:

**[Biconvex ADMM for Sensing-Aided XL-MIMO Channel Estimation at the THz Frequency Band](/publication/vlachos-sensing-thz-xlmimo-2026/)**
*Evangelos Vlachos and George C. Alexandropoulos*

The base station's own radar output warm-starts the channel estimator, and the recovered delay profile comes back as a sensing output at no extra pilot cost. The paper is part of our work on [channel estimation for mmWave and THz massive MIMO](/research/channel-estimation/).

### Belgrade, 19 November: EUSOME training on advanced air mobility

At the **EUSOME Training on Advanced Air Mobility**, hosted by the Institute Mihajlo Pupin, I give two lectures:

- **ISAC systems**: how cellular networks can sense drones while they communicate.
- **UAV communications: standards and protocols**: Remote ID, 3GPP support for UAVs, the command-and-control link and U-space services, seen from the radio side.

The training is part of the [EUSOME](/projects/eusome/) project, and the project's plenary meeting follows on 20 November.
