## Key Contributions
- Development of a framework for hybrid beamforming that explicitly accounts for hardware imperfections, including phase noise and amplitude distortion.
- Formulation of an optimization problem to jointly maximize energy efficiency and spectral efficiency, considering the impact of hardware imperfections.
- Provision of numerical results demonstrating the performance trade-offs between energy and spectral efficiency under various system configurations and imperfection levels.
